# PC Remote

Control Geometry Dash on this tablet from your PC.

1. Open the main menu: the address is shown at the bottom (e.g. `192.168.1.25:7777`).
2. On the PC (same Wi-Fi), open `http://192.168.1.25:7777` in Chrome or Edge, or open `gd-remote.html` and type the IP.
3. Space / W / Up = jump, A/D or arrows = left/right (platformer), L = player 2, Esc = pause, R = restart.
